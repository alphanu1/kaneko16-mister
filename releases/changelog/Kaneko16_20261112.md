## Rotation no longer knocks a CRT out of sync

Setting **Rotation** to anything but Off used to make a 15 kHz CRT lose sync.
The core was switching the analog output over to the HDMI scaler while
rotating, and the scaler runs at HDMI scan rates. The analog output now always
carries the core's own 15 kHz picture.

| Setting | CRT / analog (Direct Video on or off) | HDMI |
|---|---|---|
| Off | normal | normal |
| Auto / CW 90 / CCW 90 | normal (stays in sync) | turned 90 |
| 180 | **turned 180** | turned 180 |

- **180 now works on a CRT**, including Direct Video. It is done inside the
  core rather than in the HDMI framebuffer, so a vertical cabinet whose monitor
  is mounted the other way gets an upright picture. This is the setting
  Explosive Breaker on a CRT needs.
- **CW 90 and CCW 90 work on every game**, including the horizontal ones
  (Blaze On, Magical Crystals), for portrait HDMI screens. A quarter turn
  cannot be carried on a 15 kHz raster, so on a CRT those settings deliberately
  leave the picture alone.
- With Direct Video on, the quarter turns are off on HDMI too, because the
  scaler is bypassed in that mode.

## CRT Adjust

New OSD page: **H-Size** (-12..+10, 1% per step), **H-Position** (-48..+42 px)
and **V-Shift** (-8..+7 lines), shown once CRT Adjust is On. This is rmonic79's
MiSTer-CRT-Adjust, wired the same way as in the Namco NA1/NA2 core. The picture
moves inside native sync, so the CRT holds lock while you adjust. A rotated
HDMI picture is never affected; an unrotated HDMI picture follows the adjustment
while it is On. Off, the default, is the previous output, unchanged.
