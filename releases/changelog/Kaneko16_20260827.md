## Sound

**Explosive Breaker is audible at last, and the gain was never the problem.**
A silent YM2149 does not read mid-scale — jt49's output is unsigned and its
silence is **zero** — so centring it by a fixed midpoint put a constant −1024
into the mix. Explosive Breaker keeps both YM2149 volumes at zero for its whole
soundtrack, so that offset was permanent: −24576 after the mix gain, three
quarters of the negative rail. The OKI rode on it into the rail and clipped.

The path is now AC-coupled, as the board's own coupling capacitors are — a
first-order high pass with a corner near 7 Hz. With the offset gone the level
is back to **×4**, which is where the arithmetic says unity is: jt6295's sample
is 14-bit, so four times it fills a 16-bit output.

Two gain increases were made before this, chasing a symptom whose cause was a
DC level and not a level. Both are undone.

**Separate Music and SFX volumes**, 20% to 200% in tens. Which chip each moves
is per board:

| Board | Music | SFX |
|---|---|---|
| Blaze On, Wing Force | YM2151 | OKI |
| Explosive Breaker, Magical Crystals | 2× YM2149 | OKI |

**On Explosive Breaker the split is not real.** It plays everything through the
OKI, so SFX moves the whole soundtrack and Music moves nothing audible. That is
the hardware.

Both start at 100%: `status` defaults to zero, so position 0 is what a fresh
boot gets, and the menu runs 100% up to 200% before wrapping to the quiet end.

## Video

**Rotation can now reach the analog output.** `VGA_SCALER` is raised while the
framebuffer is in use, so analog follows the scaler when rotating and keeps the
direct path — and its lower latency — when not.

**It cannot work with Direct Video on.** That mode puts the core's own timing on
the VGA pins and bypasses the scaler by definition, so there is no rotated image
on that path to carry. Direct Video has to be off to rotate on a CRT.

**A rotation setting saved by a pre-180 core is honoured again.** The select
widened from `status[25:24]` to `[26:24]` when 180 was added, giving bit 26 a
meaning it had never had — so an old config could read "CW 90" as an unused
code and resolve it to Off, with the menu still showing CW. Codes 5 to 7 now
fall back to what their low two bits meant before.

## Known issues

- **Rotation on a CRT through VGA**, reported by a third party as working on
  `Kaneko16_20260824` and not on `Kaneko16_20260825`, with HDMI unaffected
  throughout. **Unexplained.** For CW and CCW the core's rotation outputs are
  identical between those builds, and HDMI working rules out the stale-bit
  fault above, which would have disabled both outputs. Either something outside
  the core differs on that machine — Direct Video, `vga_scaler` in MiSTer.ini,
  a firmware version — or the build remembered as working is not the one
  assumed. The `VGA_SCALER` change above is new to this release and is the
  first thing to test.
- Tier 2 (Shogun Warriors, B.Rap Boys) is a separate core and not in this
  build.
